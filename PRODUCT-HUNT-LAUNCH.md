## 4. Maker's first comment

Post this as the first comment right after the launch goes live:

---

Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I built QuickBill POS after watching small shop owners in India struggle with two things: billing apps that die the moment the internet drops, and hardware POS machines that cost more than a month's profit.

QuickBill is an Android app that turns any phone or tablet into a full POS:

- **Works fully offline** — every bill is saved on the device, so billing never stops when the network does
- **Amount-first keypad** — type ₹120, tap Add, done. No mandatory product catalog
- **GST bills** — CGST/SGST shown separately with your GSTIN (5%, 12%, 18%, or 0%)
- **UPI QR with the exact amount** — on screen and printed on the bill; works with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM
- **Bluetooth thermal printing** — any 58mm/80mm ESC/POS printer, zero drivers
- **Sales history & exports** — filter by Cash/UPI/Card/Split, reprint bills, export to Excel/CSV

Pricing is simple: ₹99/month or ₹999/year (~$1.2/month), unlimited bills, 3-day free trial. There's also a ₹3,199 kit that bundles a Bluetooth printer and paper rolls so a shop can start billing the same day.

I'd love your feedback — especially from anyone who's run retail billing in low-connectivity environments. Ask me anything! 🙏
